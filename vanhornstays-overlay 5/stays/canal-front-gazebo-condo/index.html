A renovated two-bedroom, two-bath corner unit that feels like a standalone home, a short walk from Las Olas Boulevard and the Riverwalk. It's one of two separate units on the property, and you rent it on its own.

There's a private gazebo on the canal, a patio with a grill, and a desk if you need one. Vaulted ceilings and big windows keep the inside bright, and both bathrooms are en suite.

It works well for wedding groups, family visits and boat show weekends. Parking in the driveway is free, and the beach is about two miles away.

Trash and recycling

Fort Lauderdale collects household garbage twice a week and recycling once a week, between 7 a.m. and early evening, Monday through Saturday. Yard waste goes out weekly, and bulk trash is picked up once a month.

Pickup days depend on the address, so message us and we'll tell you where the bins are and which days they're emptied. Please bag your trash, and don't leave loose bags on the ground next to the bins.

If a pickup is missed, the city's Neighbor Call Center at (954) 828-8000 is open around the clock.

Things to do nearby

  • Las Olas BoulevardRestaurants, coffee, galleries and shops, and an easy walk from both homes. The strip runs all the way east to the beach.
  • RiverwalkA paved path along the New River through downtown, with benches, boats and places to eat. It connects to the Museum of Discovery and Science, Stranahan House and the Broward Center.
  • Water TaxiA hop-on, hop-off day pass on boats that run along the New River, the Intracoastal and out to the beach. There's a stop near Las Olas shops and restaurants. Boats usually run from about 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Stranahan HouseThe oldest surviving building in Fort Lauderdale, built in 1901 beside the New River at 335 SE 6th Ave. Tours run most days, and hours change with events, so check ahead.
  • Museum of Discovery and ScienceA big hands-on science museum with an IMAX theater, at 401 SW 2nd St. It's usually open Monday to Saturday 10 to 5 and Sunday noon to 5. A good rainy-day plan.
  • Bonnet House Museum and GardensA 35-acre estate of gardens and a 1920 house full of art, near the beach at 900 N Birch Rd. Open Tuesday through Sunday and closed Mondays. Reserving tickets online is easiest.
  • The beachLas Olas Beach is about 2 miles from the homes, and Fort Lauderdale Beach is about 3. Hugh Taylor Birch State Park, with trails and a lagoon, sits just across A1A.

Hours and prices change. Check each place's website before you head out.
