Trash and recycling

The City of Pittsburgh picks up trash once a week and recycling every other week. Put bins out no earlier than 6 p.m. the night before and no later than 6 a.m. on pickup day, and bring them back in by 10 p.m. that night.

Recycling goes in loose, clean and dry, with no bags. If a holiday falls on or before your pickup day, collection slides one day later. The pickup day for the address can be looked up at pgh.st, or message us and we'll tell you.

If a pickup is missed, call 311 (412-255-2621).
