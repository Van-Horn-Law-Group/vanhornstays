Shadyside, Pittsburgh

Two suites in the same building, a short walk from Walnut Street's shops and cafes and 0.7 miles from Carnegie Mellon.

Shadyside is one of Pittsburgh's walkable neighborhoods. Walnut Street's shops, cafes and restaurants are close, and Carnegie Mellon, Phipps Conservatory and the stadiums are a short drive.

The two suites are in the same building and share a deck, so they work for families, reunions and game weekends. Book one, or book both and you have room for 12.

Things to do nearby

  • Walnut StreetThe heart of Shadyside, with more than 80 shops and restaurants on and around the street. The three blocks at the southwest end are the busiest. Dogs are welcome at lots of the patios and stores.
  • Ellsworth AvenueA quieter strip a few blocks over with coffee shops, neighborhood bars and small stores.
  • Phipps Conservatory and Botanical GardensGreenhouses and gardens inside Schenley Park, about 2 miles away, with seasonal flower shows all year. Check the website for hours, which change with the season.
  • Carnegie museumsThe Carnegie Museum of Art and the Carnegie Museum of Natural History are next door to each other in Oakland, a short drive or bus ride from Shadyside.
  • Schenley ParkBig park with trails and open lawns, right next to Phipps. Good for walking the dog.
  • Carnegie Mellon and the University of PittsburghCarnegie Mellon is 0.7 miles from the suites, and Pitt is about 1.4 miles.
  • Game dayPPG Paints Arena, PNC Park and Acrisure Stadium are all a short drive away. On Steelers weekends, book early.
