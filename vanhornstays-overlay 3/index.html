Before you book

  • Dogs: welcome at all four homes. The fee and limits differ, and each page lists them.
  • Check-in: self check-in at every home, so there are no keys to pick up. Check-in is after 3 PM and check-out is by 11 AM.
  • Parking: free at all four. In Fort Lauderdale that's one car in the driveway. In Shadyside each suite has one dedicated spot in the back, and street parking is available.
  • Working while you travel: the Fort Lauderdale homes have a desk and wifi at 150 to 195 Mbps.
  • More than one home: the two Fort Lauderdale rentals are two separate units on one property, each booked on its own, and they sleep 11 together. The two Shadyside suites are in the same building and sleep 12 together. Ask us about booking both.
